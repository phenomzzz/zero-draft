Panel strip

A row of panels where hovering or focusing one widens it and narrows its neighbours by distance. The others zoom in slightly, shift with a little parallax and darken. Looking at a panel never chooses it: choosing is a separate button inside the open panel. On touch, the first tap opens a panel and the second uses it.

Base component: expanding-strip (annnimate catalog), recreated from scratch. Values as tuned in the booking build. All components
