Fullscreen menu

Open the menu. A curtain wipes down while the page behind dims and drifts up, the rows rise into their masks, rules draw and an arched photo window opens. Hovering a row fills it from the edge the pointer came through and swaps the photo with a wipe in the direction of travel. Closing plays the same timeline backwards, faster, then scrolls to the chosen section.

Base components: fullscreen-slide-menu and hoverable-list (annnimate catalog), recreated from scratch. Values as tuned in the booking build.
