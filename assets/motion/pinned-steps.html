Pinned steps

The section pins while the visitor scrolls through three steps. Scroll position picks the step, with a little hysteresis so it does not flicker at a boundary, and snaps to the nearest step. The indicator follows scroll continuously. Each change wipes the new photo in from the direction of travel while the old one drifts and dims, and the copy, caption and counter change on the same beat. Clicking a tab wins over scroll until the visitor arrives or scrolls again.

Base component for the photo change: step-wipe (annnimate catalog), recreated from scratch. The pinning, snap, hysteresis and indicator are our own. Values from both builds. All components
