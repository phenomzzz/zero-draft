Scroll header

Scroll down: the header leaves once you pass about half a screen, and comes back as soon as you scroll up. It also stays while it holds keyboard focus, or while the pointer is near the top of the window, and its colours follow the band underneath it. Base component: hide-header (annnimate catalog), recreated from scratch. Values as tuned in the booking build.

A light band

Scroll down more than 14 pixels and the header hides. Scroll up 40 pixels and it returns. The uneven pair keeps small accidental movements from flashing it.

A dark band

While a dark section sits under the header, the header switches to its dark theme. Section bounds are measured at most every 200 ms and after layout changes, never on every frame.

Another light band

Press Tab to move into the header: it stays visible while it holds focus.

A second dark band

Move the pointer to the top 90 pixels of the window to call the header back without scrolling.

The end

That is the whole behaviour.
