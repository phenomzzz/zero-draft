Motion kit

Nine motion components as single HTML files, with the values they shipped with in two production websites. Copy the mechanism, restyle it with your own tokens. Add ?motion=reduce to any page to see its reduced-motion behaviour.

  • Magnetic buttonButton and label lean toward the pointer, spring back on leave.Base: magnetic-button
  • Filter gridFlip reflow, cards join a beat later, sliding filter highlight, counting total.Base: flip-filter
  • Side sheetDetail panel with dim, staged content, previous/next, scroll lock and focus return.Base: detail-drawer
  • Pinned stepsScroll-driven steps with snap, hysteresis, a scrubbed indicator and directional photo wipes.Base for the wipe: step-wipe; pinning is our own
  • Split revealCharacters gather from the centre on scroll; lines rise in masks once.Base: character-appear, text-reveal
  • Opening frameA rounded window opens to full screen while the headline parts around it.Base: capsule-hero
  • Scroll headerLeaves on scroll down, returns on scroll up, follows the band beneath it.Base: hide-header
  • Fullscreen menuReversible curtain timeline, rows that fill from the entry edge, wiping preview.Base: fullscreen-slide-menu, hoverable-list
  • Panel stripPanels widen by distance from the one in view; viewing never chooses.Base: expanding-strip

“Base” names are components in the annnimate catalog (annnimate.com). Every file here is a from-scratch recreation, written without annnimate’s code, markup, styles or media, then adapted and tuned in our own sites. annnimate is not affiliated with this kit. See README.md in this folder.
