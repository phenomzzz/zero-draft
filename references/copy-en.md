# English copy: contextual diagnostic leads

Apply the [shared safeguards and model tells](copy-deslop.md) first. Confirm US/UK or another variant from the brief; preserve brand vocabulary and specialist terms.

## Inspect meaning before vocabulary

- Staged openings and redundant closers ("In today's fast-paced world", a closing moral): cut when they repeat rather than inform.
- Unsupported significance, superlatives and borrowed authority ("industry-leading", "experts agree"): verify or flag. Never invent an expert, result or mechanism.
- Empty benefit riders and abstract nouns (", empowering teams to unlock value"): name the supported benefit, actor or relationship.
- Chatbot remnants and generic reassurance ("Rest assured", "We've got you covered"): remove when foreign to the source voice.

Vocabulary leads, not bans: seamless, elevate, unlock, empower, supercharge, effortless, game-changer, crafted, curated, bespoke, nestled, journey, delve, cutting-edge, thoughtfully, quietly. Assess meaning, frequency and audience; a raiding guild's Cutting Edge achievement is a precise domain term.

## Conventions

Keep one variant throughout: US usually closes em dashes and puts periods/commas inside quotes; UK often uses single quotes, spaced en dashes and logical punctuation. Follow the brand's serial-comma and heading-case choice consistently. Curly quotes, contractions and deliberate rhetoric that fit the voice are not tells.

## Website decisions

Use atmospheric expression where it earns attention; make the offer, location, capacity, terms and actions explicit nearby. Do not restate the same promise in every heading. Match CTA wording to the next state: an availability inquiry must not imply a confirmed reservation. Errors explain the recoverable condition and next action without blaming the visitor.

Illustrative edits, not client facts:

| Original | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Discover an unparalleled world of unforgettable adventures." | Replace with verified details or a distinctive supported line | Interchangeable praise does not explain the offer |
| "E-bikes with helmet and lock included, 200 metres from the lake." | Keep if sourced; localize spelling/units | Useful decision information |
| "It's not a confirmed booking yet; the team will check availability." | Keep/refine | Contrast states a real status distinction |
| "Book now" opening an inquiry form | Refine to "Check availability" if that describes the flow | Predictable action and truthful status |
