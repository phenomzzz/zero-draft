# Contextual copy editing: English and PT-BR

## Scope and routing

Use for website copy creation, de-slop, substantial content review or a targeted wording repair. Improve reader understanding, credible specificity and brand fit. Do not infer AI authorship or optimize detector scores. Use this shared contract plus only the relevant locale reference: [English](copy-en.md) or [PT-BR](copy-pt-br.md). Mixed-language pages keep approved domain terms; translation is a separate task unless requested.

For substantial direction changes use [contextual-deslop.md](contextual-deslop.md) to research sector, audience and visitor task; reuse an applicable project baseline for small edits. Copy review alone does not require a visual redesign or live publication.

## Establish what the copy must accomplish

Identify locale (including English variant), audience, brand voice/sample, placement, visitor decision, factual source and intended action. Distinguish expressive brand copy, descriptive marketing, practical information and functional microcopy. A poetic hero can sit beside literal booking controls; do not impose government-service tone on an atmospheric brand.

For a new sector or disputed recommendation, inspect peer pages in the relevant market and authoritative content guidance. Record observed wording and context, not proven conversion from prevalence. Do not copy a competitor's slogans or descriptions. Author notes and approved business records take precedence over generated drafts; resolve material conflicts without silently choosing the attractive claim.

## Factual and semantic safeguards

Before editing, identify names, numbers, dates, prices, units, sources, quotes, actors, causal claims, uncertainty, conditions, exceptions, commitments and current status. Keep the inventory light for a sentence; map sources for a complex page.

A stylistic rewrite must preserve those meanings. Never invent specificity, reviews, statistics, facilities, experiences, guarantees, scarcity, progression or availability. Do not drop necessary qualifications to sound confident. Flag unsupported claims separately; do not strengthen them or silently delete commitments. Ask only when an essential gap blocks faithful work; otherwise make a conservative edit and name the gap.

Changing wording does not authorize changing link destinations, tracking, schemas, control semantics, CMS IDs, form field names or translation keys. Preserve quotations and legal wording unless their editing is in scope. Distinguish an inquiry from a booking, and selecting from replacing, continuing or submitting.

## Model copy tells

LLM copy (GPT/Codex and Claude) converges on a few structures in both languages. They are leads to justify or replace, not a ban list; one instance can be the right line. Read across the page, where repetition shows.

- **Slogan syntax:** a short declarative headline ending in a period, one word in accent color or italic ("Built different.", "Feito para durar."), repeated section after section.
- **Antithesis:** "Not X. Y.", "No X. No Y. Just Z.", "It's not about X, it's about Y."
- **Tricolons:** three adjectives, three benefits, three clipped sentences; every list exactly three long.
- **Inflated or hushed register:** GPT-leaning drafts inflate (unlock, seamless, supercharge, emoji bullets); Claude-leaning drafts go quietly literary (considered, thoughtfully, quiet craft, made with care). Attribution is anecdotal; both are defaults, not voice. Locale vocabulary is in the locale files.
- **Punctuation habits:** em dashes where a comma, colon or period works; a colon in every heading; Title Case (wrong for PT-BR).
- **Fake specificity:** round unsourced numbers ("10,000+ teams", "99.9%"), invented testimonials, logo strips, ratings, "trusted by".
- **Frames:** "Whether you're X or Y", "From X to Y", "Ready to…?" headings, "Say goodbye to…", CTAs like "Get started" that do not name the next state.

Derive instead: headlines name the thing, place, person or outcome from the source; rhythm follows the content (one line, or the two or four items that actually exist); proof is real or absent. Replacing a formula with another formula everywhere (every heading becomes a question) is the same failure.

## Editing method

1. Diagnose the reader consequence: ambiguous action, missing information, unsupported claim, repetition, misplaced tone or generic language. A listed phrase is only a lead.
2. Choose keep, refine or replace. Approved voice and intentional rhetoric may justify a familiar expression. No mandatory synonym substitution, punctuation ban or fixed paragraph length.
3. Rewrite the smallest useful span. Prefer a concrete supported detail; keep an honest general statement if the source lacks detail. Do not fabricate quirks, mistakes, anecdotes or slang.
4. Check progression and rhythm across the section, then facts and action semantics against the original. Stripping tells can leave uniformly clipped, slogan-like prose; avoid that.
5. If implementing, inspect rendered wrapping, nav/button labels, small screens, long CMS text and accessible names. Do not cut critical conditions to fit; repair the layout.

Scale passes to the task. A substantial review benefits from a compact original/proposed/reason table, source gaps and deliberate keeps. Do not expose a long drafting loop or manufacture a humanity score.

## Acceptance and evidence

Assess accuracy, task clarity, voice fit, specificity, locale naturalness and rendered fit separately; a factual failure invalidates a stylish rewrite. Phrase checks find candidates, not quality or authorship. Never claim conversion uplift, compliance or preference without evidence. Use [copy-evaluation.md](copy-evaluation.md) for regression probes and trial method.
