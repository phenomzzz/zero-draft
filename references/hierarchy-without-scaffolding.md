# Hierarchy without decorative scaffolding

Creation and review guidance for repeated hairlines, tracked or monospace uppercase eyebrows and arbitrary section numbers, or for orientation lost after removing them. These devices are not inherently defects; apply contextual de-slop. At creation use the section openings below and give every label, rule and number a job from the start ([delivery-workflow.md](delivery-workflow.md)); a review pass alone does not shape generation. Mono micro-labels are part of the convergent house grammar ([design-profile.md](design-profile.md)) and a common default in demo libraries; treat their presence as a choice that needs a reason.

## Diagnose the job and the cumulative pattern

Inspect a representative page sequence, not one section. For each device name its job: orientation, taxonomy, sequence, grouping, comparison, action affordance or deliberate brand expression. Flag redundant labels, unreadable tiny caps, meaningless numbering and the repeated rule + eyebrow + number + heading recipe. One occurrence proves nothing about quality or authorship.

Keep table/schedule separators, genuine numbered steps, navigable chapters and categories that add information. Judge link underlines separately; they signal clickability. A deliberate editorial identity may keep the system selectively. Descriptive section headings are not redundant eyebrows; keep them and their semantic levels.

## Replace the function before removing the device

| Needed support | Alternatives | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identify subject | Descriptive heading, specific opening sentence, recognizable image/caption | Poetic heading alone hides the practical topic |
| Separate sections | Whitespace rhythm, restrained surface change, image boundary, composition change | Huge gaps or alternating bands become the next template |
| Group content | Proximity, alignment, shared container or heading | Mobile stacking detaches related items |
| Establish hierarchy | Type scale/weight/width, reading order, line length, image/text proportion | Oversized repeated headlines replace tiny repeated labels |
| Orient or show progress | Descriptive anchor nav, active state, real step title, meaningful count | Sequence disappears or becomes decorative dots |
| Differentiate topic | Subject-specific photography, data or illustration | Generic icons replace arbitrary numbers |
| Identify action | Clear wording, contextual placement, selective contrast, predictable affordance | Links lose rest-state affordance or depend on hover |

Do not substitute glass panels, gradients, oversized type or extra motion as the new universal default. Then recompose geometry per [composition-and-affordance.md](composition-and-affordance.md).

## Build sections without eyebrow, header block or enumerator

A recurring failure: agents cannot open a section without eyebrow + big title + intro, or without "01 / 02 / 03". Start from the content's own first element instead:

| Opening | How it works | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subject-led | The image or object leads; a caption-weight descriptive heading sits beside or under it | Bike photo, then "Aro 29, quadro M, 21 marchas" |
| Statement as heading | The descriptive `h2` is the first sentence, near body scale and weight, followed directly by content | "Three ways to reach the lake from São Paulo." then the routes |
| Items carry the hierarchy | No section title; each item's name is the heading and the layout shows the group | Menu, team, product list |
| Data or quote leads | A figure, map, schedule or testimonial opens; a heading-level line explains it | "4.9 from 312 rentals" with source |
| Composition change marks the boundary | Shift in grid, scale, media proportion or surface signals a new section, no rule needed | Full-bleed image after a text column |
| Real sequence only | Ordered list with step titles; numbers only where order matters | Booking steps |

Keep a semantic heading for every section, even when visually quiet. Vary openings by content, not rotation.

## Minimum label size

At 1920×1080, text a visitor must read (labels, captions, metadata, navigation, button text) computes to at least 14 CSS px, body at least 16 px; nothing meaningful drops below 12 px at any viewport. Uppercase tracked labels: at least 14 px, tracking at most 0.08em, contrast at least 4.5:1. Measure computed `font-size`, not the stylesheet.

## Reference research

For a substantial direction decision, research per [research-and-direction.md](research-and-direction.md), starting with the sector and visitor task. For each reference section record URL/date, viewport/state, whether each device is absent, selective or present, and what does its job instead: how the subject is understood without an eyebrow, boundaries without a rule, orientation without numbers, grouping when stacked or without images. Limit absence claims to inspected sections. Text extraction cannot establish casing, borders or spacing; mark failed visual access instead of inventing observations. Reuse an applicable project baseline for small repairs.

## Record and verify

Record element, job, problem, reference mechanism, adaptation, keep/refine/replace and evidence. Verify with [composition-evaluation.md](composition-evaluation.md): topic clarity, grouping when stacked, heading semantics, anchor orientation, label readability, link/focus affordance, no-image and reduced-motion fallbacks. Removal must not reduce comprehension.
